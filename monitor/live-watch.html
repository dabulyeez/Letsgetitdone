Stock prices and settings

Crypto is live from Coinbase with no setup. For live stock prices, get a free key at finnhub.io (email sign-up, no card), then paste it here. It is saved only in this browser and only sent to Finnhub.

Alerts fire when a price reaches your stop or target, or comes within 3% of your stop. On iPhone, notifications work after you tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there.
