3. Lock out every IP that isn't yours

Gmail, Shopify and banks don't let you block one IP. You lock them out by signing everyone out and making the next sign-in need something only you have.

  1. Google / Gmail: open Your devices, sign out every device that isn't yours. Then change your password and turn on a passkey or 2-Step Verification with the Google prompt, not text messages. To see IPs, open Gmail on a computer, scroll to the bottom, click Details next to "Last account activity". For the most protection, join Advanced Protection.
  2. Gmail forwarding: check Forwarding and Filters. Thieves often add a rule that copies or hides your mail. Delete any you didn't make.
  3. Shopify: Settings, then Users, then your account, then Security. Log out all other sessions, turn on two-step authentication, and remove any staff or app you don't recognize.
  4. Apple ID: account.apple.com, then Devices. Remove anything that isn't yours.
  5. Microsoft / Outlook: Recent activity shows IPs and a map. Click "Secure your account" on anything that isn't yours.
  6. Bank, PayPal, Cash App: sign out all devices in security settings and call the number on the back of your card about any red IP.
  7. Your home Wi-Fi: log in to your router, change the admin password and the Wi-Fi password, then look at the connected devices list for anything you don't own.
  8. Report it: file at IdentityTheft.gov and with local police. Attach the report you copy from this page.
