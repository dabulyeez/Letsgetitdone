## 3. After you send

1. Save the confirmation page or number for each complaint. Keep them off the board and in your private vault.
2. The CFPB sends your complaint to Navy Federal, and they usually answer within **15 days** (60 at most). You'll see their answer in your CFPB account and can say whether you're satisfied.
3. Check your credit reports for free at annualcreditreport.com. When a credit bureau gets an identity theft block request with your FTC report, the law gives it **4 business days** to block the loan (Fair Credit Reporting Act section 605B). If the loan is still showing about 2 weeks after your letters arrived, tell Claude and we'll take the next step with that bureau.
4. A Better Business Bureau complaint is optional and only after these.
